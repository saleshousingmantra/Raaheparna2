What draws buyers to Moshi

Moshi sits in the Pimpri Chinchwad region, home to the Pune International Exhibition and Convention Centre and placed along the Pune Nashik highway corridor. Industrial and commercial belts, educational institutions and new residential clusters surround it. That mix usually keeps housing demand steady, driven by working families, students and professionals.

Dudulgaon, next door to Moshi

Dudulgaon lies beside Moshi and Charholi, near the Indrayani River. Raahee Parna is located here, so residents get a calmer setting while schools, hospitals, shopping and IT parks remain within roughly 15 minutes. Our guide to getting around from Raahee Parna lists the distances in detail.

Checks to make before you pay

Whatever the project, a few steps protect you:

  • Confirm the RERA registration on the official MahaRERA website
  • Look at the developer's record and delivered projects
  • Compare carpet area and the total package, including stamp duty, registration and GST
  • Visit at different times of day to judge traffic and surroundings
  • Read the agreement closely, including payment schedules and maintenance charges

Living in it or renting it out

Many buyers in Moshi plan to live in their home and value a peaceful campus. Others look at rental income from professionals and students working nearby. Rent depends on the micro-market, the quality of the project and how well it is looked after. Good amenities, security and a well planned campus generally make a home easier to rent or resell, which is why we describe the facilities inside Raahee Parna in such detail.

Think about the long term

A home is usually held for years, so look at how the area may change as much as at today's price. Roads, schools and hospitals already operating nearby support everyday life, and a well run society keeps a building appealing over time. Ask the developer how maintenance will be managed once the project is handed over.
