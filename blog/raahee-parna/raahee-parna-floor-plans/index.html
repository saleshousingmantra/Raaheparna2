What to look for in a plan

  1. Is the living room wide enough for your furniture, and where does the light come from?
  2. Are the bedrooms and bathrooms placed so doors do not clash?
  3. Where is the kitchen, and is there room for a utility area?
  4. How many flats share each lift lobby on the typical floor?

Ask the team which wing and floor each carpet area is in. Plans are indicative and subject to change as per approvals. For detailed plans, leave your details.
