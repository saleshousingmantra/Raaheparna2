How to verify the registration

  1. Open the Raahee Parna page on the MahaRERA website, or search for the number PR1260002602355 on maharera.maharashtra.gov.in.
  2. Check the project name, the promoter, the address and the dates shown for the project.
  3. Match the registration number with the one printed on the agreement and the brochure.

Why it matters

A RERA-registered project has its approvals, timelines and details on public record. The registration page also shows the possession timeline declared for the project, so read it there and confirm it with the team in writing.
