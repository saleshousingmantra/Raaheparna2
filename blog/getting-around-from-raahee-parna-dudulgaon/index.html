Getting Around from Raahee Parna: Dudulgaon's Schools, Hospitals and Hubs

By Raahee Corp · 27 October 2026 · 3 min read

The right address makes every day easier, so it pays to look beyond the flat itself. Raahee Parna stands in Dudulgaon, close to Moshi in the Pimpri Chinchwad (PCMC) region of Pune, near the Indrayani River. This guide sets out what lies around the project and how long typical trips take. All times are approximate and come from the project's location map.

A calmer corner of PCMC

Dudulgaon lies between the established areas of Moshi and Charholi, with Alandi to the east. It feels quieter than the busy heart of Pune, yet markets and services are never far. The Indrayani River gives the surroundings a green, open character that is hard to find in a city address.

Schools within easy reach

Parents will find plenty of options close to Raahee Parna:

  • Aditya International School: about 3 to 5 minutes
  • New Orchid International School: about 4 to 7 minutes
  • Priyadarshani School: about 7 to 10 minutes
  • Radcliffe School in Chovisawadi, Silver Birch International School and City Pride School: about 8 to 12 minutes
  • Apex International School and RKL Galaxy International School in Wadmukhwadi: about 10 to 15 minutes

Healthcare close to home

Medical help is near. Kamlesh Multispeciality Hospital and Jijau Mother and Child Care Hospital are about 3 to 5 minutes from Raahee Parna, and Dhanashree Multispeciality Hospital in Moshi is around 5 to 7 minutes. Bliss Hospital, Tulip Hospital and Sunrise Hospital lie within roughly 5 to 10 minutes, while larger facilities such as Accord Hospitals are about 15 to 20 minutes away.

Shopping and leisure

For everyday needs, DMart and Reliance Trends and Digital are roughly 5 to 10 minutes from the project. For a bigger day out, Town Plaza, Silverian Mall and Spine City Mall are about 10 to 15 minutes away, so weekly shopping and weekend plans need little travel.

Colleges and universities

Students are well placed too. Rajmata Jijau Shikshan Prasarak Mandal College of Pharmacy in Dudulgaon is just 2 to 3 minutes away. MIT Arts, Commerce and Science College, Modern College of Pharmacy and Dnyanvilas College of Engineering are around 5 to 10 minutes, Dr D Y Patil Technical Campus is about 15 to 20 minutes, and Ajeenkya DY Patil University is roughly 20 to 30 minutes.

Reaching work

Professionals can reach several employment hubs from here. The Business Gate is about 5 to 10 minutes away and Charholi IT Park about 10 to 15 minutes. Talwade IT Park takes around 30 to 40 minutes, while Hinjewadi IT Park and Kharadi are farther, at roughly 60 to 70 minutes depending on traffic. The location therefore suits people who work in the PCMC belt or have flexible hours.

What this means for daily life

Put together, the figures paint a practical picture. School runs are short, the nearest hospitals are only minutes away, and groceries and fashion stores are close enough for a quick trip. Even the larger malls are a modest drive, which keeps weekend plans relaxed. For families, that mix of short everyday distances and a calm riverside setting is the real appeal of Dudulgaon.

Visit before you decide

When you come to see Raahee Parna, try the trips you would make every day: to the office, to school and to the nearest hospital. Do it once at peak hour and once on a weekend, and walk the nearby streets to see the shops for yourself. A visit tells you far more about the pace of the neighbourhood than any brochure can.
