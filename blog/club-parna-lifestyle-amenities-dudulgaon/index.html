Inside Raahee Parna: Club Parna, the Pool and 40+ Amenities

By Raahee Corp · 27 October 2026 · 3 min read

A home is more than four walls, and the facilities around it shape daily life as much as the floor plan does. Raahee Parna, a project by Raahee Corp in Dudulgaon near Moshi in PCMC, Pune, plans for that with over 40 amenities. The campus covers 3.5 acres, holds 686 homes in 7 towers of 14 floors, and offers 2, 2.5 and 3 BHK layouts. Here is a zone-by-zone look at what residents can expect.

Club Parna: where neighbours meet

The social centre of the project is Club Parna. It houses a multi purpose hall, a cafeteria, a library and a co working space, so a quiet read, a family function or a work call can all happen without leaving the campus. Outside the clubhouse, the swimming pool has separate pools for kids and adults, and a poolside lounge sets the mood for relaxed evenings.

Sports for every energy level

Raahee Parna gives active households a wide choice. The sports plan lists a cricket turf, badminton and squash courts, a basketball court, a climbing wall, table tennis and carrom, and a riffle shooting area. A jogging track and skating rink link these zones, giving children and adults a safe, traffic-free place to run, skate and play.

Wellness spaces for body and mind

Health is covered with a mix of workout and recovery options:

  • An AC gym and fitness area
  • A zumba studio for group sessions
  • Yoga and meditation spaces
  • An open air gymnasium
  • An oxygen park and an acupressure pathway

With these on the doorstep, a regular fitness habit becomes much easier to keep.

Spaces for children, seniors and celebrations

Every age group has a corner of its own. Children get a dedicated play area, while a senior citizen sit out gives elders a comfortable place to chat. For get-togethers, the amphitheatre, party lawn and open party lawn, plus a gazebo, BBQ area, camping fire area and selfie point, make birthdays and festivals simple to host at home.

Green surroundings and a temple

Landscaping is built into the plan. A landscape garden and walking pathways thread through the campus, and a temple inside the project serves daily prayers and festivals. The nearby Indrayani River adds to the calm of the Dudulgaon setting.

Daily conveniences and security

Small conveniences make a big difference to everyday comfort, and Raahee Parna includes several:

  • A Wi Fi zone and co working space for remote work
  • A common car wash centre and a driver room
  • EV charging facility and an EV van for society use
  • Provision for a salon and a day care centre
  • A common cooking area for functional activities

On the safety side, the plan lists a secure main gate, CCTV in common areas, video door phones, power backup, fire safety systems and a garbage chute. Together they keep the community running smoothly when families are out at work or school.

Designed for everyday use

The aim is not to impress on paper but to be used. A resident can start the day on the jogging track, send the children to the play area, take a work call in the co working space and end the evening at the poolside lounge, all within a short walk of home. That everyday ease is what the amenity plan is built around.

How to judge an amenity list

A long list only helps if the facilities get used. When you compare projects in Dudulgaon and Moshi, ask how close each facility is to your tower, whether every age group is catered for, and how upkeep will be handled. At Raahee Parna everything sits inside one 3.5 acre campus, so the amenities stay within walking distance of every home.
