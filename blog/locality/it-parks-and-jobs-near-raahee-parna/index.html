Who this location suits

The location suits people who work in the PCMC belt or have flexible hours. If your office is in Hinjewadi or Kharadi, test the commute at peak hour before you decide, because traffic changes the time a great deal.

Test your own commute

  1. Open Raahee Parna on Google Maps and set your office as the destination.
  2. Check the time at your usual leaving hour, not just off-peak.
  3. Ask colleagues who live in Moshi and Charholi how they travel.
