Questions

Your questions on Raahee Parna

Straight answers on location, sizes, pricing and registration. Want more? Our team will call you.

Where is Raahee Parna?

Raahee Parna is in Dudulgaon, close to Moshi in PCMC, Pune, near the Indrayani River. DMart, Reliance Trends, schools and hospitals are roughly 3–10 minutes away.

What home sizes are offered?

You can choose a 2 BHK (685–707 sq ft carpet), 2.5 BHK (832–894 sq ft) or 3 BHK (966–1,008 sq ft) in the B, C and D wings.

What does a flat at Raahee Parna cost?

Total package prices begin at ₹58.53 Lakh for a 2 BHK, ₹68.14 Lakh for a 2.5 BHK and ₹79.06 Lakh for a 3 BHK, covering agreement cost, stamp duty, registration and GST. Rates may change without prior notice; check the price list above.

How large is the project?

It has 686 homes in 7 towers of 14 floors on 3.5 acres, with over 40 lifestyle amenities.

Is Raahee Parna registered under RERA?

Yes. Its MahaRERA registration number is PR1260002602355, which you can verify at maharera.maharashtra.gov.in.
