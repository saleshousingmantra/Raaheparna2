Questions

Raahee Parna, answered

Quick answers on location, sizes, price and registration. Need more? Our team will call you.

Where is Raahee Parna located?

Raahee Parna is in Dudulgaon, near Moshi in PCMC, Pune, close to the Indrayani River. DMart, Reliance Trends, schools and hospitals are within about 3–10 minutes.

Which configurations are available?

2 BHK (685–707 sq ft carpet), 2.5 BHK (832–894 sq ft) and 3 BHK (966–1,008 sq ft), across the B, C and D wings.

What is the price of a flat in Raahee Parna?

Total package prices start from ₹58.53 Lakh for a 2 BHK, ₹68.14 Lakh for a 2.5 BHK and ₹79.06 Lakh for a 3 BHK, including agreement cost, stamp duty, registration and GST. Rates are subject to change without prior notice; see the price list above.

How big is the project?

686 homes in 7 towers of 14 floors, spread over 3.5 acres with 40+ lifestyle amenities.

Is Raahee Parna RERA registered?

Yes. The MahaRERA registration number is PR1260002602355. You can verify it at maharera.maharashtra.gov.in.
